AdjFlow for Adobe Premiere Pro
AdjFlow is a streamlined CEP panel for Adobe Premiere Pro designed to drastically speed up your editing workflow. Generate adjustment layers, create color mattes, and apply effects or custom keyframe presets with a single click.

🚀 Key Features
Instant Adjustment Layers & Color Mattes: Create new adjustment layers or color mattes directly above your timeline selection with a single click.

Automatic Effect & Preset Application: Effector presets applied through AdjFlow are automatically added directly to the newly placed adjustment layers.

Alt-Key Cut Detection & Transition Mode: Hold down the Alt key to trigger a dedicated mode that automatically places adjustment layers across your cut points, allowing you to easily define custom transition lengths.

User Presets with Keyframe Support: Save custom effect combinations along with animation keyframes for rapid reuse—bringing powerful preset management directly into your workspace (similar to Excalibur).

Vertical & Adaptive Layout: Designed specifically with a vertical panel layout that docks seamlessly into Premiere Pro's sidebar workspaces.
